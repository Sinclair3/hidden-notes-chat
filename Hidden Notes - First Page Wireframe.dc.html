Secret unlock
The search bar is the trigger. Type ##open and press Enter — the screen instantly swaps to the hidden chat. Otherwise it searches notes normally.
Backup unlock
Shake the phone (or tap the ◎ logo 5×) for the same result — for when typing isn't safe. Both swap instantly, no animation, no trace.
Real app, not a shell
Notes genuinely create, edit, delete & search. The disguise has to work to be convincing — the + button and cards are all live.
